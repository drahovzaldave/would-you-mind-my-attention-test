The bigger picture

Your emergency brake:
the 3-second rule.

Anger, fear and excitement can push us to react before we check. The attention economy rewards content that captures attention and encourages quick engagement.

1. StopPause for 3 seconds.
2. ThinkNotice the emotion. Don’t let it decide.
3. VerifyCheck a reliable, independent source.
Before you share, ask:
Who is the original source? Is there independent confirmation? Is the date and context correct?

A short pause does not guarantee accuracy—but it creates space to make a more deliberate choice.

FINE FOR EXCEEDING
COGNITIVE SPEED

Your feed wants a reaction.

Fast scrolling leaves little time to assess evidence. Even students trained to analyse information can be influenced by urgency, emotion and repetition.

Your fine: Take three seconds before you share. Verify the claim before you amplify it.

STOP · THINK · VERIFY

What this test can—and cannot—tell us

This short activity shows how you responded to five examples in a timed setting. It does not, by itself, prove that time pressure caused an error or that algorithms changed your thinking. To test that, researchers would need a comparison condition and a larger sample.
